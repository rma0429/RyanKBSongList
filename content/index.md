🚀 How to Navigate / 如何使用本站

🔍 Search (搜尋):

search for any song or artist instantly.

點擊放大鏡，快速搜尋歌名或歌手。

📂 Browse (瀏覽):

Use the Explorer open on the left sidebar to navigate through folders 

使用左側點開的 Explorer (檔案瀏覽器) 查看不同分類的資料夾

🔗 Graph View (關聯圖):

Check the graph on the right to see how songs and artists are connected.

查看右側的關聯圖，探索歌曲與歌手之間的連結網絡。
